The Agronomic Math

Space-to-Soil Fusion Algorithms

How Field Shift transforms coarse NASA satellite land-surface models into actionable, plot-level farm advice.

Δ

Microclimate Soil Gap

Soil Gap = Local Soil (%) - NASA GWETTOP (%)

Measures local deviation from the regional 50 km satellite pixel. Detects localized drought pockets or poor drainage tiles that satellite passes average out.

r

Pearson Correlation Tracking

r = Σ((L - L̄)(M - M̄)) / (σ_L × σ_M)

Evaluated across a rolling 16-pair buffer. A high positive $r$ means the plot naturally follows macro rainfall. Low or negative $r$ indicates artificial irrigation or greenhouse decoupling.

💧

Multi-Factor Irrigation Index

Need = Base Deficit × (1 - Rain7) × Root Buffer

Prevents over-watering by combining root moisture deficit with NASA's 7-day cumulative rainfall offset and deep root-zone wetness (`GWETROOT`).

Engineering Rigor

Dual-Core FreeRTOS Architecture

Zero dynamic heap allocations in steady-state. 15 decoupled telemetry channels running on the Beken BK7258 SoC.

Streaming NASA TLS Client (Zero-Heap Overhead)

Connects directly to power.larc.nasa.gov over TLS. Ingests ~2.2 KB agro-climatology payloads with chunked parsing, maintaining system free heap at ~168 KB indefinitely.

Sliding 14-Day Temporal Window

NASA MERRA-2 satellite land models carry a 2-to-3-day publication lag. Field Shift automatically scans backwards 14 days to find the newest complete valid record.

Zero-NTP HTTP Date Header Clock Synchronization

Lacking a battery-backed RTC, Field Shift parses the standard HTTP Date header returned in the NASA TLS response to synchronize the system clock without an external NTP client.
